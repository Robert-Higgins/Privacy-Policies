# Letter Drop! Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 22 September 2026

**Developer:** Tiggrat Studio (Robert Higgins)
**Contact Email:** tiggratstudio@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed by Tiggrat Studio ("I", "me", or "my") when you use my mobile game "Letter Drop!" (the "Game"). I am a solo developer committed to protecting your privacy.

The Game is free to download and play, with optional in-app purchases and advertisements.

**What changed in this version:** version 1.2 of the Game adds player profiles and friends. If you sign in to Google Play Games, the Game now creates an online account for you and publishes a public player profile, which other players can see. This is described in Sections 2.3 and 3.5.

## 2. Information I Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- No registration is required to play the Game, and I never ask for your name, email address or contact details.
- **Username:** you may choose a username for your player profile. It is public (see Section 2.3), so please do not use your real name or other personal information in it. Usernames are checked against a list of offensive words.
- **Friend codes:** if you add a friend, you enter the friend code they have given you.
- If you make in-app purchases, payment information is processed by the Google Play Store (subject to Google's privacy policy). I never see or receive your payment details.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Game progress data:** your gameplay progress, high scores, experience and level, in-game gem balance, items you own or have equipped, and whether you have purchased ad removal or a gem pack.
- **Advertising identifiers:** the advertising service used by the Game (Google AdMob) collects your device's advertising ID along with device and usage information, in order to serve and measure ads. See Section 3.1.
- **Google Play Games data:** if you sign in to Google Play Games, your achievements, leaderboard scores and a copy of your game save are stored on your Google Play Games profile. See Section 3.3.
- **Online account identifier:** if you sign in to Google Play Games, the Game uses that sign-in to create an account for you with Google Firebase. Firebase assigns you a random user ID and links it to your Google Play Games player ID. See Section 3.5.
- **Local storage:** your save data is stored on your device in encrypted form.

### 2.3 Your Public Player Profile

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the Game publishes a player profile that **any other signed-in player of the Game can view**, including on friends lists and leaderboards. It contains only:

- your username, profile icon and title;
- your high score, the total of your ten best scores, the number of games you have played, your level and total experience;
- the achievements you have unlocked;
- your friend code, and the time the profile was last updated.

Your profile never contains your gem balance, your purchases, your save data, your email address or your Google account name.

The Game also stores your friends list and any pending friend requests. A friend request shows the recipient your username. Your friends can see that they are on your friends list.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services:

### 3.1 Google AdMob

- Displays advertisements within the Game. These take three forms: a banner shown during play, a full-screen ad shown after a game ends, and optional rewarded video ads you may choose to watch in exchange for in-game gems.
- AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, device information and usage data in order to serve, measure and (where permitted) personalise advertising.
- If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the Game shows you a consent prompt before any personalised advertising is served, and you can change your choice at any time from the Game's settings.
- You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings.
- Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google's advertising partners: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

### 3.2 Google Play Billing

- Handles all in-app purchases, including gem packs and ad removal.
- Processes your payment information. I do not have access to your payment details, and I receive only a confirmation that a purchase completed.
- Your purchase history for the Game is held by Google and is used to restore your purchases when you reinstall or sign in on a new device.
- Google Play Terms: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms

### 3.3 Google Play Games Services

- Stores your achievements and leaderboard scores.
- Stores a copy of your game save (Google Play Saved Games), so that your progress, gems and purchased items follow you to a new device. This copy contains only game data – no name, email address or contact information is added by me.
- Provides the sign-in used to create your online account (Section 3.5).
- Requires an internet connection and a Google Play Games sign-in to sync. Sign-in is optional; the Game is fully playable without it, using local saves only.
- You can manage or delete this data through your Google account and Google Play Games settings. See Section 8.

### 3.4 Google Play In-App Review

- The Game may ask Google Play to show its standard in-app review prompt. Any review you write is submitted to Google Play, not to me, and is published under your Google Play profile name.

### 3.5 Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore)

- Used only if you sign in to Google Play Games, to provide player profiles and friends.
- **Firebase Authentication** creates your online account from your Google Play Games sign-in. It stores a random Firebase user ID and your Google Play Games player ID. The Game does not request your email address from it.
- **Cloud Firestore** stores your public player profile (Section 2.3), your friend code, your friends list and your pending friend requests. This data is held in Google's data centres in the United States.
- As with any online service, Google's servers process your IP address and basic technical information in order to deliver the service.
- The Game does not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or any other Firebase tracking or advertising feature.
- Unlike the other services in this section, I control this data: Google stores it on my behalf as a data processor.
- Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

- Game progress is stored locally on your device in encrypted form, and is retained until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
- If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is also stored on Google's servers and is retained according to Google's policies until you delete it.
- Your online account, public profile and friends data (Section 3.5) are kept for as long as your account exists, and are deleted when you ask me to delete them (see Section 8). Removing a friend deletes the link from both players' lists straight away.
- Apart from the Firebase data in Section 3.5, I do not hold a copy of your data.
- Advertising and purchase data are held by Google under Google's own retention policies.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

I do not:

- Sell or rent your personal information.
- Share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
- Collect personal information beyond what is described in this policy.

Data is shared only:

- With other players, limited to your public player profile and friend requests, as described in Section 2.3.
- With Google, which stores the Firebase data in Section 3.5 on my behalf.
- As required by the third-party services described in Section 3 (Google Play, Google Play Games, AdMob), which collect data directly as independent controllers of that data.
- To comply with legal obligations, or to protect my rights or safety.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. While it is a word game suitable for general audiences, I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Players cannot send each other messages; the only information shared between players is the public profile described in Section 2.3. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me at the address in Section 11 and I will delete it, and assist in having it removed from the other services concerned.

## 7. Your Rights in the EEA and the UK

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the following applies.

**Controller.** For the player profile, friends and online account data described in Section 3.5, the controller is Tiggrat Studio (Robert Higgins), contactable at tiggratstudio@gmail.com, and Google acts as my processor. Google acts as an independent controller for advertising, Play Games Services and Play Billing data, as described in its own privacy policy.

**Legal bases.** Game progress, saved-game data, your online account, your public profile and your friends list are processed to provide the Game and the online features you have chosen to use by signing in (performance of a contract). Personalised advertising is processed only with your consent, gathered through the prompt described in Section 3.1. Non-personalised advertising, basic fraud prevention and filtering offensive usernames rely on legitimate interests in funding a free game and keeping it safe for its players.

**Your rights.** You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. For your profile, friends and online account data, contact me directly and I will act on your request. Requests relating to advertising, saved games, achievements or purchases must be directed to Google; I will help you identify the right route if you contact me.

**Withdrawing consent.** You can withdraw consent to personalised advertising at any time from the Game's settings, or through your device's ad settings.

**International transfers.** The Firebase data in Section 3.5 is stored in the United States, and Google may process the other data described in Section 3 outside the EEA and the UK. These transfers are covered by the safeguards set out in Google's data processing terms and privacy policy, including Standard Contractual Clauses.

**Complaints.** You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk).

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

- **Ad personalisation:** change your choice in the Game's settings, or in your device or Google account settings.
- **Your public profile:** you can change your username, icon and title in the Game at any time. The change replaces the previous version on the server.
- **Friends:** you can decline friend requests and remove friends in the Game at any time.
- **Deleting your online account:** email tiggratstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Letter Drop! Account Deletion" and your friend code (shown on your profile screen), and I will delete your online account, public profile, friend code, friends list and friend requests within 30 days. Your local save and your Google Play Games data are not affected; see below for those.
- **Local data deletion:** uninstalling the Game, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes all data stored on your device.
- **Cloud save deletion:** uninstalling does *not* delete your Google Play Games data. To remove it, open the Google Play Games app, go to Settings, and delete the Play Games account data for Letter Drop!. This deletes your saved game, achievements and leaderboard scores for the Game.
- **In-app purchases:** manage, review or request refunds for purchases through the Google Play Store.
- **Playing offline:** you can play without signing in to Google Play Games, in which case no game data leaves your device, no online account is created and no profile is published.

## 9. Security

Save data is encrypted on your device, and all network communication used by the Game is carried out by Google's own software over encrypted connections. Access to profile and friends data is restricted by server-side security rules: only you can change your own profile, and friend requests can only be read by the player they were sent to. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.
